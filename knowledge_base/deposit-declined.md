# Why was my deposit declined?

If a payment fails, you'll see "Payment failed. Please try again." and no money is added to your balance.

## Common reasons

- Your bank or card issuer blocked the payment. Some banks block payments to gaming apps by default.
- The card details, expiry date or security code were entered incorrectly.
- There are not enough funds on the card.
- You reached a daily deposit limit set in Responsible Gaming settings.

## What you can do

- Tap **Try again** and check your card details.
- Try another payment method, such as Apple Pay or PayPal.
- Contact your bank and ask them to allow the payment.

A declined payment can appear as pending on your bank statement for a few days before your bank removes it.
