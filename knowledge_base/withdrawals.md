# How do I withdraw my winnings?

You can withdraw your winnings from your balance to PayPal or to a bank account.

1. Tap your balance, then **Withdraw**.
2. Choose PayPal or bank transfer and enter the amount.
3. Confirm your identity if asked.

The minimum withdrawal is $10. Withdrawals usually arrive within 1 to 5 business days. Bonus cash cannot be withdrawn.
