# How do I add funds to my Blitz account?

You can add funds from the **Deposit** screen at any time.

1. Tap your balance at the top of the lobby.
2. Tap **Deposit** and choose an amount.
3. Choose Apple Pay, PayPal or a debit or credit card, and confirm.

## Limits

- The minimum deposit is $5.
- Maximum per deposit: Apple Pay $15, PayPal $40, card $100.
- Daily deposit limits you set in Responsible Gaming settings always apply.

Deposits are free and appear in your balance right away.
