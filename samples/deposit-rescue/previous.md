# Deposit error screen

## Summary
When a deposit fails, the player sees an error screen and can try again.

## How it works
1. The player starts a deposit and chooses a payment method.
2. If the payment fails, the screen shows "Payment failed. Please try again."
3. The player taps **Try again** and restarts the deposit from the beginning.

## Rules and limits
- Maximum per deposit: Apple Pay $15, PayPal $40, card $100.
- The minimum deposit is $5 for every method.
- Daily deposit limits set in Responsible Gaming settings apply.
- Blitz charges no fees on deposits, whatever the method.

## Availability
- iOS and Android, all app versions.
