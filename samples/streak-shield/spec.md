# Streak Shield

## Summary
Streak Shield protects your daily streak when you miss a day, so one busy day doesn't wipe out weeks of play.

## Why it matters for players
- Long streaks feel great and unlock bigger daily rewards.
- Life happens: a missed day shouldn't reset weeks of progress.

## How it works
1. Play at least one game a day to grow your daily streak.
2. Every 7 days in a row, you earn a Streak Shield. You can hold up to 2 at a time.
3. If you miss a day, a Shield is used automatically and your streak continues.
4. The next morning, a notification tells you a Shield was used.

## Rules
- Shields are free and cannot be bought.
- A Shield is never used on a day you already played.
- Your streak day ends at midnight in your phone's time zone.

## Availability
- iOS and Android, app version 5.10 or later.
- All players, including those who only play free games.

## Settings
- Turn streak reminders on or off in Settings > Notifications.

## Support notes
- Players can see their streak history in Profile > Streaks.
- Changing the phone's time zone more than once in a week pauses Shield earning for 7 days, to prevent abuse.
- Shields cannot be restored manually by agents.
