# PRD: Fast Withdrawal (v1)

**Owner:** Payments squad · **Epic:** PAY-1870

## Summary

Players can choose a faster withdrawal option to their bank account.

## Who gets it

- Players in the US who have completed identity verification.
- iOS and Android.

## How it works

1. Player opens **Wallet** and taps **Withdraw**.
2. Player enters an amount and picks **Fast Withdrawal**.
3. Player taps **Confirm**.
4. Funds arrive within 1 business day.

## Limits and fees

- Minimum withdrawal: $20.
- Maximum withdrawal: $1,000 per day.
- Fee: $2.99 flat.
- Standard bank transfer (ACH) is free and takes 3 to 5 business days.
