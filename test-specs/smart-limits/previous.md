# PRD: Deposit Limits (v1)

**Owner:** Responsible Gaming squad · **Epic:** RG-410

## Summary

Players can set a limit on how much they deposit.

## Who gets it

- All players in the US.
- iOS, Android and web.

## How it works

1. Player opens **Settings > Responsible Gaming**.
2. Player taps **Deposit Limit** and picks daily, weekly or monthly.
3. Player enters an amount and taps **Save**.

## Rules

- Lowering a limit takes effect right away.
- Raising a limit takes effect after 24 hours.
- Limits apply to every payment method.
- Players can pause their account for 7, 30 or 90 days from the same screen.
